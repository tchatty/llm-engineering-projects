# Sustainability of AI

### Application Objective: 
The Sustainability of AI application aims to promote a better understanding of the environmental impacts of artificial intelligence through extracting insights from a curated corpus of literature. 

The application also applies a custom methodology to quantify the environmental impacts of every operation performed by the application from on-device computation to LLM calls. This methodology combines the use of tools such as Code Carbon and Ecologits for this computation.
